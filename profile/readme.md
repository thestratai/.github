## ⚡ Beyond Lagging Charts: The TRW Shift

Most trading software tells you what already happened—lagging indicators, noisy news feeds, and chaotic charts. **TRW (Trading & Research Wing)** builds decision intelligence platforms that show you what is likely to happen next, audit your trade setups before you risk capital, and fuse price action, order flow, and news sentiment into absolute market clarity.

## 🚀 Flagship Product Ecosystem

### 🔮 1. Strat AI — Quantitative Decision Terminal (NSE Equities & F&O)
**[Strat AI](https://stratai.live)** replaces emotional guesswork for Indian market traders with an embedded multi-agent risk officer and predictive analytics suite.

* 🔍 **15-Step FIND Scan**: Autonomously scans macro trends, technical microstructure, market regimes, relative strength vs NIFTY 50, CVD/OFI order flow, volume profiles, and 19 institutional chart patterns.
* 🛡️ **VERIFY Risk Auditor**: Stress-tests entry levels, enforces a strict $1.5 \times \text{ATR}$ stop-loss floor, minimum Risk-to-Reward ratios ($1:2$ swing, $1:1.3$ intraday), and unleashes a **Bear Agent (Devil's Advocate)** to find hidden setup flaws.
* 📈 **Predictive Ghost Lines**: Renders 10-minute candle price trajectories directly on your chart using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression, backed by real-time $R^2$ confidence scores.
* ⚔️ **DEBATE & Consensus Engine**: Spawns Bull and Bear agents to debate trade thesis viability under the supervision of an objective Judge Agent.
* 🧠 **Fused Conviction Score (1–100)**: Combines $70\%$ technical signals and $30\%$ LLM sentiment analysis, featuring instant breakout anomaly detection via DeepSeek v4 Pro.
